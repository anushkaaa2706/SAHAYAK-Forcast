THE MISSION

From Environmental Signals to Early Action

Severe weather develops through rapidly changing atmospheric and surface conditions. SAHAYAK brings these signals together to identify where hazardous conditions may intensify and support earlier action.

The Challenge

Severe weather risk depends on multiple interacting signals that can change rapidly.

  • ⬢Rapid moisture accumulation
  • ⬢Heavy precipitation events
  • ⬢Atmospheric instability
  • ⬢Cloud and storm development
  • ⬢Strong winds and gusts
  • ⬢Terrain and local vulnerability

INTELLIGENCE LIFECYCLE

Predict → Explain → Prioritize → Warn → Verify → Respond → Learn

01

Predict

AI models analyze weather, atmospheric, terrain, and satellite signals to identify emerging severe-weather risk.

02

Explain

Explainable AI reveals which factors are driving the risk at each location.

03

Prioritize

Rank locations by risk severity and exposure to focus limited resources.

04

Warn

Generate actionable warnings with clear severity and recommended actions.

05

Verify

Field officers confirm conditions and feed observations back into the system.

06

Respond

Coordinate evacuation, road closures, and relief deployment across agencies.

07

Learn

Outcomes refine the models — the system improves with every event.
